# 🎓 RUSSIAN SCHOLAR

An interactive full-stack Russian language mastery and survival platform designed specifically for international students, government scholarship holders, and **Open Doors** olympiad winners preparing for their academic and student journey in Russia.

---

## 🌟 Welcome to Russian Scholar
Moving to a new country is an exciting adventure, but navigating daily life in Russia requires functional, immediate survival language skills. Even if your university program is taught in English, daily activities—such as grocery shopping at *Pyaterochka*, finding your dormitory room, checking in at passport control, or buying medicine at an *Apteka* (pharmacy)—are conducted entirely in Russian.

**Russian Scholar** bridges this gap using advanced AI-driven conversational scenarios, real-time voice synthesis, 15-minute structured daily lessons, interactive grammar lectures, city living guides, vocabulary decks, and comprehensive alphabet training to build your confidence before you even board your flight.

## 🚀 Core Features & Recent Updates

### 1. ⏱️ Structured 15-Minute Daily Study Routine
* **Paced 30-Day Curriculum:** A structured 15-minute daily study module designed to fit into busy student schedules.
* **Interactive Countdown Timer:** Built-in timer keeps you on track while studying.
* **Three-Part Daily Breakdown:**
  * **Alphabet Letters:** Native audio playback for target letters.
  * **Vocabulary List:** High-yield words with normal and half-speed slow audio pronunciation.
  * **High-Yield Dialogues:** Practical context tips and real conversational sentences.
* **Streak & XP Rewards:** Completing your daily routine awards +10 XP and maintains your daily learning streak.
* **Mobile Optimized:** Fully fluid layout crafted specifically for mobile phone screens of all sizes.

### 2. 🗣️ Interactive Survival Voice Scenarios (AI Tutor)
Roleplay real-world dialogues with a persistent, context-aware AI tutor powered by Gemini:
* **🛒 Grocery Shopping at Pyaterochka:** Practice cashier loyalty card questions ("*Karta est'?*"), buying grocery staples, and checking out.
* **💊 Pharmacy Assistance:** Describe symptoms (colds, headaches) to get the correct over-the-counter medicine.
* **🎓 University Enrollment:** Submit registration documents, verify scholarship letters, and receive your student ID (*studencheskiy bilet*).
* **🚕 Taxi Booking & Airport Customs:** Speak with Yandex-style taxi drivers and border agents.
* **🏠 Dormitory Check-in:** Coordinate with dormitory commandants (*komendant*) and locate your room.

### 3. 📝 Intelligent Grammar Correction
* **Emerald Grammar Cards:** If you make a mistake in Cyrillic spelling or case endings, the AI highlights correct usage in dedicated green assistance cards.
* **Visual Distinctions:** Clear distinction between general conversation output and grammar feedback.

### 4. 🎙️ Dual-Speed Voice Synthesis (TTS) & Audio
* **Audio Playback:** High-fidelity Russian Text-To-Speech with normal speed and slow half-speed play button for accent and pronunciation accuracy.
* **Speech-To-Text Input:** Voice input support for fast, hands-free speaking practice.

### 5. 🏙️ Community Forum & Russian City Guides
* **Student Community Hub:** Connect with fellow scholars heading to Russia, ask questions, share tips, and upvote discussions.
* **Comprehensive City Guides:** Explore essential guides for top student destinations including Moscow, Saint Petersburg, Kazan, Novosibirsk, Tomsk, Nizhny Novgorod, and Ekaterinburg.

### 6. 📚 Full Learning Suite
* **🔤 Alphabet Master:** Complete Cyrillic alphabet chart with visual breakdowns and audio pronunciation.
* **⚡ Word-of-the-Day Decks:** Daily vocabulary items tailored for student life.
* **🎯 Grammar Lectures & Tests:** Modules covering noun genders, plurals, and case systems with instant quiz feedback.
* **🔥 Streak & XP Engine:** Complete lessons, maintain your daily streak, earn XP, and unlock Scholar badges.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
Make sure you have [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (v18 or higher) installed.

### Installation
1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/russian-scholar.git
   cd russian-scholar
   ```

2. Install dependencies:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. Configure environment variables in `.env` (refer to `.env.example`):
   ```env
   GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key
   VITE_SUPABASE_URL=your_supabase_url
   VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=your_supabase_anon_key
   ```

4. Start the development server:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) to view the app in your browser.
